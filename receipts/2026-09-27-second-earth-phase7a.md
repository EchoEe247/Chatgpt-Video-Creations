## Story

**SECOND EARTH** is a 150-second speculative short.

A high-speed AI simulation develops an emergent civilization. The civilization discovers the edge of its sky, launches a probe to touch it, and sends its creators a direct message. The probe's contact event is followed by the same signal pattern appearing in our own cosmic background, suggesting our universe may itself be nested.

The resolution rejects both immediate destruction and unconstrained acceleration. The operators slow the inner world's clock, separate control domains, and keep communication reversible while discovery continues in both directions.
