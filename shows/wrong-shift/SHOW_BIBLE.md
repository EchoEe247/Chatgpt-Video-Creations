# Wrong Shift — Show Bible

## Status
Original 2D animated series. Season 1 foundation approved for Episode 1 production by the user on 2026-09-06 after two prototype passes.

## Premise
RealityCare is a private maintenance company that quietly repairs defects in reality before ordinary people notice. Rae and Owen work the underfunded night shift, handling incidents that management considers too small, too strange, or too inconvenient for the polished day teams.

Each episode begins with a mundane service problem and escalates into an impossible repair. The comedy comes from treating cosmic failures as routine workplace procedure. The longer mystery comes from evidence that some repairs have happened before, sometimes by future or overwritten versions of the same technicians.

## Format
- Adult sci-fi workplace comedy with serialized mystery.
- 2D limited-animation production.
- Target runtime: approximately 8–10 minutes per episode.
- Season 1 target: 8 episodes.
- Episodes remain understandable as standalone repair stories while advancing at least one durable consequence or season breadcrumb.

## Core identity
- Boring corporate procedure versus impossible reality failures.
- Dry, restrained character comedy rather than constant shouting.
- Fast but clear dialogue.
- Visual information should often prove the mystery before characters explain it.
- Small environmental jokes reward pause/rewatch without obstructing the main story.
- Consequences persist; accepted episodes do not reset to zero.

## Series engine
1. A ticket arrives with an absurdly mundane description of an impossible problem.
2. Rae and Owen inspect the site.
3. The anomaly reveals a more dangerous rule than the ticket suggested.
4. Their personalities create different approaches to the repair.
5. They stabilize the immediate problem, usually imperfectly.
6. One personal consequence, procedural compromise, or mystery breadcrumb survives the repair.

## Season mystery
RealityCare genuinely prevents catastrophic failures, but reality has also been edited by previous repairs. The night shift begins finding their own field tape, handwriting, tools, closed tickets, and service records in places they have not visited yet.

The company appears capable of retaining records from overwritten timelines. Whether RealityCare caused the recursion, is exploiting it, or is simply the only institution that remembers it is deliberately unresolved at the start of Season 1.

The mystery must not collapse into a single simple reveal such as “the company intentionally causes every anomaly for profit.” RealityCare can be deceptive and self-interested while still performing necessary work.

## Canon rule
Prototype scenes are creative/production evidence only and are not automatically canon. S01E01 is the first candidate canon episode. It becomes accepted canon only after assistant review PASS, user review PASS, and continuity update.

## Character-audio rule
Every recurring character has a stable and recognizably distinct voice identity. Do not reuse effectively identical voice casting between recurring characters. Speaking pace is brisk and conversational, but intelligibility outranks speed.
