# SECOND EARTH — storyboard

## 01 · shot-01 · 6s · Python/Pillow/NumPy · HERO

**Story job:** Hook the viewer with the impossible message before explaining the machine.

**Viewer sees:** Inside a dark computation sphere, a miniature city lights up and the words ARE YOU THERE? emerge from its street grid.

**Camera:** Fast push from black into the glowing city-core.

**Motion:** City lights converge into letterforms while the core pulses. Sparse particles condense from darkness into a visible simulated world.

**Palette:** near-black, ice cyan, one warm amber fault

**Transition:** hard emergence → macro silicon

**Audio/narration:** At 02:17, a city inside our machine asked us not to turn it off.

**Review at:** 2.2, 5s local

**Fail if:** message feels like generic title card; city scale is unclear; hook motion too subtle
